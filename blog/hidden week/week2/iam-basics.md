IAM is one of the most critical systems in cloud security.

Most real-world breaches come from:
- over-permissioned roles
- leaked credentials
- weak trust policies

Least privilege sounds simple, but in practice:
- teams over-grant permissions for speed
- permissions accumulate over time
- audits happen too late
