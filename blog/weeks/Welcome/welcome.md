Hi everyone! I've been wanting to get this blog out for a really long time, and everytime I think of starting I end up putting it on hold.

I've been super passionate about cybersecurity for over a year now and for just as long I've wanted to have a place to share my thoughts. As a Gen-Z student, I'm constantly flooded with information. Whether it's through the news, podcasts, social media, or conversations in the classroom at a tech heavy school, people are always talking about what's happening in the world and I've always wanted an outlet to do the same.

Truthfully, I'm not entirely sure what this blog will actually become. Maybe it will be about cybersecurity news. Maybe it will be about a new project I'm working on. Or maybe it will be about a problem I spent way too long trying to solve. 

Welcome to Week 1 of my blog. <br>
- No defined roadmap. <br>
- No defined direction.<br>
- No defined anything.

Actually, let's remove the world defined from our library altogether.

Life isn't defined. Careers aren't defined. Success isn't defined. Most of the meaningful things in life happen somewhere between having a plan and figuring it out as you go. This blog is no different.

So here's to getting started, wherever this ends up going.

I hope you enjoy hearing my inside voice.
