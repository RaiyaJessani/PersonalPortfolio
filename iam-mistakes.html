IAM mistakes I keep seeing in real systems

Identity and Access Management (IAM) is one of those systems that looks simple until you scale it.

The most common issue I’ve seen is over-permissioned roles combined with unclear service boundaries. In practice, this creates lateral movement paths attackers can exploit.

The fix is rarely technical alone — it’s a combination of least-privilege design, good defaults, and continuous auditing.
